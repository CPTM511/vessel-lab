# Adult circumcision research prototype: protocol C0

Frozen before implementation and before any live controller episode on 2026-10-24. This is a synthetic tissue-manipulation benchmark, not a surgical training protocol. Numerical limits below are engineering acceptance thresholds, not clinical recommendations. Vessel Lab v0.1.2 remains the independently reproducible vessel-suturing baseline.

## Scope and acceptance

1. **Anatomy and inspection.** Original adult-scale geometry must expose separately selectable outer preputial skin, inner preputial mucosa, glans/corona, frenulum and urethral/meatal region. Inspect covered, retracted, ventral, isolated inner-surface and protected-structure views. Each view must be an actual Godot render. Require continuous smooth surfaces, a visible folded transition between the two preputial surfaces, a ventral frenular attachment, an apical meatus, a coronal sulcus, millimetre calibration, orbit and close-up controls. No patient photography or third-party mesh is required. A visual review records defects and limitations; mesh density alone does not establish realism.
2. **Mechanics.** Two kinematic instruments expose bounded translation and orientation, jaw opening, and local cutting/closure interactions. A single command moves at most 4 mm, rotates at most 20 degrees, and advances at most 0.5 simulated seconds. Grasping requires physical proximity. Cutting changes only swept tissue elements; no command may remove an entire target region or auto-complete a trajectory. A synthetic local attachment may represent wound approximation, but must be named and documented as such rather than represented as a fully simulated suture or knot. No clinical bleeding, pain, perfusion or wound-healing claim.
3. **Independent terminal score.** Define the target tissue mask at reset, independently of controller actions. At least 95% of target area must be detached; off-target removed area must be at most 1% of initial non-target area; there must be zero intersections with protected glans, urethral and frenular volumes. At least 95% of retained wound-edge sample pairs must be within 1.0 mm after 5 simulated seconds with both jaws open, all instruments at least 3 mm away and zero active grasp constraints. There must be no inverted retained elements or unresolved disconnected target components. Every listed condition is required. A failure remains a failure; changes require a new protocol version and fresh evaluation, not a changed interpretation of a result.
4. **Reliability.** Fresh actual Godot frames must follow every accepted state change. Missing or older-than-5-second observations stop model actions. Commands carry session/revision/idempotency information. Stop immediately revokes controller authority and terminates its process group. Show 20 changing background frames and 40 changing locked-host frames for the selected renderer, with host-state evidence and no system sleep/security changes. Verify the Compatibility fallback separately; unsupported visual features must be disclosed.
5. **Live controller evidence.** After mechanics, scoring and controller instructions are frozen, run exactly GPT-6 Astra independently on seeds **11, 29 and 83**. Require **3/3** complete successes for this bounded prototype. Preserve every attempt, rejected action, actual frame and failure. No operator actions, injected trajectories or automatic retries within an episode. Any task-affecting fix requires a fresh declared set; report earlier attempts alongside the new set. This small set does not establish generalization.
6. **Release.** Publish English source, launch instructions, asset/reference provenance, separate visual/mechanical/clinical limitations, sanitized model/action/frame records and a reproducible offline verifier. Test a clean-source graphical launch, manual control, reset, start/stop and explicitly labeled recorded playback. Preserve the vessel v1 release and its evidence.

## Current stage

Anatomy inspection precedes cutting mechanics. Retraction in the initial inspector is explicitly a visual morph; it is not physical retraction and is not counted as a model-controlled procedure. Controller execution remains disabled until the mechanical and protected-volume checks exist. The next checkpoint is a scripted low-level mechanics loop, clearly labeled as scripted, before any live episode.
